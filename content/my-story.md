I'm Dillian McEntyre, a horror author from Arkansas. My writing journey began after the loss of my daughter, Kya. During that difficult time, my therapist encouraged me to try journaling as a way to express what I was feeling.

Those first handwritten pages eventually grew into *Something Follows*, my completed psychological horror novel, which I continue to query. What began as an outlet became a lasting passion for storytelling.

Today, I write stories that explore fear, memory, loss, and the unsettling things that hide beneath ordinary life. *The Listener Beneath*, my supernatural folk horror novel, is forthcoming in 2027, and I'm writing the conclusion to its duology, *The Listener Above*. I hope these books are only the beginning.
