DILLIAN McENTYRE AUTHOR WEBSITE — POLISHED MONOGRAM EDITION

PREVIEW THE WEBSITE:
1. Open this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It should open in Chrome or Edge.
3. Keep the assets folder and updates.js next to index.html.

ADD YOUR AUTHOR PHOTO TO “MY STORY”:
1. Choose a portrait or headshot of yourself (ideally vertical, with your face near the center).
2. Save it as a JPG file named exactly: author-photo.jpg
3. Copy it into the website's assets folder (next to dm-mark.svg).
4. Refresh index.html in your browser. The DM placeholder will automatically be replaced by your photo.
5. When ready, redeploy the complete site folder to your existing Netlify project.
TIP: You can also upload the photo to ChatGPT and ask to have it inserted. No coding required.

PUBLISH ON NETLIFY (FREE SUBDOMAIN):
1. Sign in at https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag this entire extracted dillian-author-2026 folder into Netlify Drop.
3. Netlify will assign a free .netlify.app address.
4. If you change the website later, update the files and redeploy to the SAME Netlify site to keep your address.

POST A NEW UPDATE:
1. Right-click updates.js and choose Open With > Notepad.
2. The existing announcements are examples. Copy one complete { ... } block in the list.
3. Paste the copy at the TOP of the list; change the date, category, title and text.
4. Keep a comma BETWEEN announcement blocks; do not put a comma after the final block.
5. Save and redeploy the folder on your existing Netlify site.

NOTICE:
- This is a static author site; a click-to-publish dashboard is NOT installed yet.
- There is no live newsletter subscriber collection yet.
- Individual book designs are illustrative artwork, not official covers.
- The site uses Google Fonts when online, with local system fallbacks offline.
- Nothing here is published publicly until you deploy it.

DESIGN NOTES: The DM monogram is an original vector crest. The home background is atmospheric and abstract. Forest imagery appears only on The Listener Beneath book feature.
